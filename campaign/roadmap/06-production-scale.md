# 06 — Production & Scale

```text
Stable release
  ↓
Observability
  ↓
High availability
  ↓
Horizontal scaling
  ↓
Queue/cache optimization
  ↓
Disaster recovery
```

Focus:
- Production deployment discipline.
- SLOs/alerts and actionable observability.
- Capacity planning.
- Horizontal scaling where justified.
- Queue/cache usage for asynchronous or expensive workloads.
- Backup verification and disaster recovery exercises.
