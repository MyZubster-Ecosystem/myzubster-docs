# 04 — Payment & Escrow

Conceptual lifecycle:

`Order created → Payment initiated → Funds/status recorded → Work delivered → Release OR dispute → Reconciliation`

Focus:
- Explicit order/payment states.
- Idempotent payment operations.
- Webhook/event verification.
- Clear release and dispute transitions.
- Reconciliation and audit trail.
- Failure and retry handling.

Escrow wording is conceptual unless a specific escrow provider or implementation is confirmed.
