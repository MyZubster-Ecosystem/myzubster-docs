# MyZubster Social Series — 01–05

Five-part social explainer series for MyZubster.

## Series

1. **01 — WHAT IS MYZUBSTER?**
   - Entry point / overview
   - Canva: https://www.canva.com/d/rb4wUwgAa9oDNgU
2. **02 — FROM SKILL TO PAYMENT**
   - User flow: skill/service → marketplace → payment
3. **03 — INSIDE THE ENGINE**
   - Conceptual architecture: app → marketplace → payment gateway → network
4. **04 — TRUST & ESCROW**
   - Conceptual transaction flow: payment → funds held → work/delivery → release or dispute
5. **05 — YOUR SKILLS. YOUR PAYMENTS.**
   - Closing manifesto: skill → marketplace → payment → ownership
   - Canva source: https://www.canva.com/d/1gpYSHioKL5hkyt

## Visual direction

- Format: Instagram portrait, 1080×1350
- Diagram-first, minimal fintech/decentralized-web aesthetic
- Dark sophisticated background, high contrast, clean geometric icons
- Consistent series markers: `01 / 05` through `05 / 05`
- Avoid unsupported claims such as guaranteed protection, guaranteed privacy, trustless, or fully decentralized

## Suggested captions

### 01
**What is MyZubster?**
A marketplace concept connecting skills, services and payments in one simple flow.

### 02
**From skill to payment.**
Offer a service, connect with a buyer, complete the work and move the payment through the transaction flow.

### 03
**Inside the engine.**
The experience sits on top of a set of components that connect the marketplace, payment layer and underlying network.

### 04
**Trust & escrow.**
A transaction can be represented as: payment → funds held → work/delivery → release or dispute.

### 05
**Your skills. Your payments.**
A simpler way to connect skills, services and payments.
