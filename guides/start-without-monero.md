# How to Start With MyZubster Without Monero

Welcome! This guide will help you get started on MyZubster even if you don't have any Monero (XMR) yet.

## Step 1: Create Your MyZubster Account

1. Visit [MyZubster Gateway](https://myzubster.com)
2. Click **Sign Up** and create your account
3. Verify your email address

## Step 2: Understanding MYZ Tokens

MYZ is the native utility token of the MyZubster ecosystem. You can earn MYZ by:
- Completing bounties
- Contributing to the community
- Referring new users
- Participating in events

## Step 3: Getting Your First MYZ Without Monero

### Method 1: Complete a Bounty
Browse open bounties on the [Gateway](https://github.com/MyZubster-Ecosystem/MyZubsterGateway/issues) and complete one to earn MYZ.

### Method 2: Community Rewards
Join the [MyZubster community](https://t.me/myzubster) and participate in discussions. Active members receive periodic MYZ rewards.

### Method 3: Referral Program
Share your referral link with friends. You earn MYZ for every new user who signs up and completes their first transaction.

### Method 4: Content Creation
Create tutorials, videos, or articles about MyZubster and submit them for MYZ rewards.

## Step 4: Your MYZ Wallet

Once you earn MYZ, they are credited to your internal MyZubster wallet:
- View balance: Dashboard → Wallet
- Transfer: Wallet → Send
- Transaction history: Wallet → History

## Step 5: Making Your First Transaction

1. Go to the Marketplace
2. Browse available listings
3. Click **Buy** on an item you want
4. Confirm the transaction — MYZ will be deducted from your wallet

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need Monero to receive MYZ?**
A: No! MYZ operates on its own internal ledger. You only need Monero for XMR-related features.

**Q: How long until MYZ appears in my wallet?**
A: Bounty rewards are credited within 24 hours of completion.

**Q: Can I convert MYZ to other currencies?**
A: MYZ can be transferred to supported external wallets. Check the [docs](https://docs.myzubster.com) for current options.
