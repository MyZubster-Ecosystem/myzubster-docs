4. Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy XMR or MYZ before contributing?

No. Start with an issue that matches your skills. Never pay a deposit or share wallet recovery words to claim a task.

How do I know whether a reward is real?

Check the issue's stated currency and acceptance criteria. Treat simulated transactions, internal ledgers, and payment promises as unconfirmed until an actual wallet transaction is verifiable.

Where should I ask a technical question?

Use the issue or pull request for work-specific questions. Use the community channel for general onboarding help.

Can AI-assisted contributions be submitted?

Follow the target repository's contribution rules and disclose material AI assistance. The contributor remains responsible for validation, security, and correctness.

Need help from Pytho?

Open the MyZubster community chat and include the repository, issue number, expected result, and what you already tried.
