## How the Reward System Works

MyZubster rewards contributors with XMR for completing tasks. Here's how it works:

| Action | Reward |
|--------|--------|
| Animal/Plant registration | 0.001 XMR |
| Verify a registration | 0.002 XMR |
| Documentation contribution | 0.001 XMR |
| Valid bug report | 0.002 XMR |

**Key points:**
- Rewards are paid in Monero (XMR) — a private, untraceable cryptocurrency
- You don't need to buy XMR first — you earn it by contributing
- Payments are sent to your personal Monero wallet address
- All bounty payout addresses are verified against the project's official address on file

## Create a Free Monero Wallet

You don't need to buy anything to create a wallet. Here's how:

### Option 1: Cake Wallet (Mobile — Recommended for Beginners)

1. Go to [https://cakewallet.com](https://cakewallet.com) or search "Cake Wallet" in your phone's app store
2. Download and install the app (available on iOS and Android)
3. Open the app and select "Create new wallet"
4. Choose Monero (XMR) as the cryptocurrency
5. Write down your 25-word recovery phrase on paper — store it somewhere safe
6. Your wallet is now ready! Your address starts with 4 or 8

### Option 2: Monerujo (Android)

1. Search "Monerujo" in the Google Play Store
2. Install and open the app
3. Select "Create new wallet"
4. Save your recovery phrase securely
5. Your wallet address is displayed on the main screen

### Option 3: Feather Wallet (Desktop)

1. Go to https://featherwallet.org
2. Download the desktop app for your OS (Windows, macOS, Linux)
3. Install and run the app
4. Create a new wallet and save your recovery phrase

> **Important:** Never share your recovery phrase with anyone. Anyone who has your 25 words can access your funds. Write it on paper and keep it offline.

## First Registration → First Earnings

Here's the complete flow from zero to your first XMR payment:

### Step 1: Create Your Wallet
Follow the wallet guide above. Make sure you have your wallet address ready (it starts with 4 or 8).

### Step 2: Register on MyZubster
- Join the MyZubster community (Telegram, GitHub, or the platform of your choice)
- Introduce yourself and express interest in contributing

### Step 3: Complete Your First Task
- Pick a beginner-friendly task (animal/plant registration is the easiest)
- Follow the contribution guidelines in CONTRIBUTING.md
- Submit your work as a pull request or registration entry

### Step 4: Get Verified
- A maintainer reviews your contribution
- Once verified, you receive a confirmation

### Step 5: Receive Your Payment
- Provide your Monero wallet address to the maintainer or bot
- The reward (0.001 XMR for registration, 0.002 XMR for verification) is sent to your wallet
- Check your wallet app to see the payment arrive

> **Tip:** Your first payment might take a few hours to a day, depending on maintainer availability. Be patient!

## FAQ for Absolute Beginners

**Q: Do I need to buy Monero first?**
No! You earn XMR by contributing. The wallet is free to create.

**Q: Is Monero safe?**
Yes. Monero is a well-established cryptocurrency with strong privacy features. Your transactions are private and untraceable by default.

**Q: What if I lose my recovery phrase?**
If you lose your 25-word recovery phrase and don't have a backup, you will lose access to your wallet permanently. Write it down on paper and keep it in a safe place. Do not store it digitally (no photos, no cloud, no notes app).

**Q: Can I use any wallet, or does it have to be a specific one?**
Any standard Monero wallet works. Cake Wallet, Monerujo, and Feather Wallet are recommended for beginners.

**Q: How much is 0.001 XMR worth?**
Monero's value fluctuates. The important thing is that you're earning it by contributing, not by buying.

**Q: Do I need a GitHub account?**
For code/documentation contributions, yes. For simple registrations, check with the community on which platform to use.

**Q: What if I make a mistake in my contribution?**
Just open a new PR or submit a correction. Maintainers will help you fix it. Everyone starts somewhere!

**Q: Can I earn more than 0.001 XMR?**
Yes! The more you contribute, the more you earn. Verified registrations (0.002 XMR), bug reports (0.002 XMR), and documentation contributions (0.001 XMR) all add up.
