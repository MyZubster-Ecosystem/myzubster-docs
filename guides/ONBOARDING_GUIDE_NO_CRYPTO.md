## What is MyZubster?

MyZubster is a community project that rewards people for **real-world contributions** — like registering a plant or animal you've seen, verifying information, or writing documentation.

Every contribution earns you **Monero (XMR)**, a type of digital money.

The key idea: **you don't need to buy any digital money to start.** You *earn* it by doing useful things.

---

## Glossary of Terms Explained in Plain Words

Don't worry — these all sound scary at first, but each is simple.

| Term | Plain Meaning | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **Cryptocurrency** | Digital money that exists only on the internet | Like Monero (XMR) |
| **Monero (XMR)** | A private, digital currency; MyZubster pays in XMR | "I earned 0.001 XMR" |
| **Wallet** | A digital "purse" that holds your XMR | A mobile app like Cake Wallet |
| **Wallet Address** | A long string of letters/numbers (like a bank account number) that identifies your wallet | `44kLzNX...hisp` |
| **Transaction** | The action of sending XMR from one wallet to another | "Daniel sent me 0.001 XMR" |
| **Recovery Phrase** | 25 secret words that can recreate your wallet if you lose it | Write these down and keep them safe! |
| **Blockchain** | The public ledger that records all Monero transactions | Like a permanent receipt book |
| **Bounty** | A task with a reward in XMR attached | "Verify a registration: 0.002 XMR" |

## Journey 1: Register an Animal → Earn 0.001 XMR

This is the *easiest* way to start.

### Step 1 — Create your wallet (5 minutes, free)
1. Install **Cake Wallet** from your phone's app store (it's free)
2. Tap **"Create new wallet"**, choose **Monero**
3. The app shows you **25 secret words** — **write them down on paper** and keep them safe
4. Copy your **wallet address** (the long string starting with `4` or `8`)

### Step 2 — Register an animal or plant
1. Go to the MyZubster website or app
2. Find the **"Register"** button
3. Fill in what you saw (species, location, when, etc.)
4. Submit

### Step 3 — Get verified
A member of the community (could be you!) will review your registration. Once it's approved:

### Step 4 — Receive your reward
Your **0.001 XMR** appears in your wallet.

```
YOUR FIRST EARNING!
──────────────
Register animal → verified → 0.001 XMR received → shows in your wallet
```

## Journey 2: Verify Someone's Registration → Earn 0.002 XMR

This task pays **double** (0.002 XMR) because it requires checking someone else's work.

1. Look through the list of unverified registrations
2. Pick one that looks correct
3. Verify it (confirm the details are accurate)
4. A maintainer double-checks your verification
5. You receive **0.002 XMR**

> **Tip:** Only claim something you're actually sure about! Accuracy matters.

## Journey 3: Contribute to Documentation → Earn 0.001 XMR

If you can write or explain things well, documentation is for you.

1. Find a documentation task or issue
2. Write or improve a guide, FAQ, or explanation
3. Submit it as a pull request
4. A repo owner integrates it
5. You receive **0.001 XMR**

## Journey 4: Spend Your Earned XMR in the Marketplace

You've got XMR — now what? You can **spend it** in the MyZubster Marketplace, which sells real services, skills, and goods.

1. Browse the marketplace with your earned XMR
2. Choose a product or a skill you want
3. Pay with your wallet (scan a QR code with your app)
4. The seller receives your XMR and delivers the item/service

```
EARNING → SPENDING  cycle
  earn XMR  →  spend in marketplace  →  keep contributing
```

## Troubleshooting & FAQ

**Q: My wallet shows 0 XMR — did something go wrong?**
A: No. Rewards are only added after a maintainer approves the task. Give it a few hours to a day.

**Q: I lost my 25-word recovery phrase!**
A: Unfortunately, without it, access is gone. That's why we insist you **write it on paper** and keep it offline. Never share it with anyone — anyone with your phrase can take your funds, and no one should ever ask for it.

**Q: What if I don't have a smartphone?**
A: Use **Feather Wallet** on your computer. Same process, just on a desktop.

**Q: Do I ever have to buy Monero to participate?**
A: **Never.** You earn XMR from the start. Buying isn't necessary.

**Q: I still need help — who do I ask?**
A: Ask in the MyZubster community channel (Telegram, GitHub Discussions). Or read the [no-XMR starting guide](GETTING_STARTED_NO_XMR.md).
