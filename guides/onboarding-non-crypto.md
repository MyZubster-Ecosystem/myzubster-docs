# Onboarding Guide for Non-Crypto Users

Welcome to MyZubster! This guide is designed for people who are new to cryptocurrency and blockchain technology.

## What is Blockchain?

A blockchain is like a digital notebook that everyone can see but nobody can erase. Every transaction is recorded permanently and transparently.

**Simple analogy**: Imagine a Google Doc that's shared with thousands of people. Everyone can view it, and once something is written, it can't be changed.

## What is a Wallet?

A digital wallet is like your bank account app, but for digital assets. It stores your:
- **MYZ tokens** (MyZubster's utility token)
- Transaction history
- Keys that prove you own your assets

**Important**: Never share your wallet's secret phrase (seed phrase) with anyone. It's like your bank password.

## Creating Your First Wallet

1. Sign up on [MyZubster](https://myzubster.com)
2. Your internal MYZ wallet is created automatically
3. You can view it under **Dashboard → Wallet**

## Making Your First Transaction

1. Earn MYZ by completing bounties
2. Go to **Marketplace** to browse items
3. Click **Buy** on something you like
4. Confirm the transaction

## Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| MYZ | MyZubster's utility token |
| Wallet | Where your MYZ is stored |
| Transaction | Sending or receiving MYZ |
| Bounty | A paid task you can complete |
| Marketplace | Where you can spend MYZ |
| Seed Phrase | Secret recovery words (never share!) |

## Safety Tips

1. **Never share your password or seed phrase**
2. **Double-check addresses** before sending
3. **Start small** — test with small amounts first
4. **Ask questions** in the community chat if unsure
