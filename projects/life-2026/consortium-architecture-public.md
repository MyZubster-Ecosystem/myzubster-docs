# MyZubster LIFE 2026 — Consortium Architecture

> **Status:** public-safe working draft, 20 August 2026. Candidate roles and organisations are exploratory and do not represent confirmed participation, endorsement, funding, pilot access or institutional commitment.

## Purpose

The working consortium model is designed around four functions that a LIFE environmental demonstration project must make clear:

1. public/institutional governance;
2. digital technology and data integration;
3. independent scientific validation and MRV;
4. a real operational demonstration environment.

A fifth function — territorial governance/end-user replication — strengthens the adoption and transfer pathway.

## Proposed core roles

| Function | Working role | Main contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Public/institutional coordinator | Applicant / coordinator | Governance, LIFE administration, policy integration, stakeholder access, replication |
| MyZubster | Technology & data partner | Platform, APIs, data architecture, dashboards, IoT/sensor integration, analytics and traceability |
| Scientific/MRV partner | Independent validation lead | Indicators, QA/QC, reference protocols, validation of sensor-derived data and impact methodology |
| Operational utility/pilot partner | Demonstration and operational-validation partner | Pilot environment, operational data, infrastructure access subject to authorization, industrial validation |
| Territorial governance/end-user partner | Replication and adoption partner | Water/territorial governance, end-user validation and transfer pathway |

The Drive working material identifies organisations such as ENEA, Hera, ATERSIR, ARPAE and reclamation consortia as possible candidates for some of these functions. **None is treated here as a confirmed consortium member.**

## Working Work Package structure

| WP | Title | Working lead model | Core scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| WP1 | Project Management & Governance | Public coordinator | Governance, LIFE reporting, financial/admin management, risk and ethics |
| WP2 | Digital Environmental Infrastructure | MyZubster | Platform, data architecture, APIs, dashboards, IoT/sensor integration, alerts and traceability |
| WP3 | Monitoring, Sensors & MRV | Scientific/MRV partner + MyZubster | MRV methodology, QA/QC, validation, environmental indicators and reference measurements |
| WP4 | Demonstration & Validation | Operational pilot partner + scientific partner | Baseline, pilot installation, operational demonstration, performance verification and user feedback |
| WP5 | Environmental & Socio-economic Impact | Scientific partner / specialist university if needed | Resource efficiency, pollution reduction, impact indicators, cost-benefit and sustainability assessment |
| WP6 | Replication, Exploitation & Policy | Coordinator + governance/end-user partner | Replication package, policy uptake, exploitation model and territorial transfer |
| WP7 | Communication & LIFE Dissemination | Coordinator / ecosystem partner | Stakeholder engagement, dissemination, LIFE communication and capacity building |

## Consortium design principles

- Keep the core consortium compact and avoid overlapping beneficiary roles.
- MyZubster should own a clearly defined digital infrastructure/data work package rather than appear only as a generic supplier.
- Scientific validation and MRV should be organisationally clear and methodologically independent.
- The operational partner should contribute a real demonstration environment and operational validation, not merely a logo or nominal pilot-host role.
- Add universities or specialist organisations only where they close a specific scientific, impact, exploitation or replication gap.
- Secure formal expressions of interest, pilot access, data availability and budget/person-month inputs before treating any role as committed.

## Immediate consortium decisions

1. Confirm the preferred eligible public/institutional coordinator.
2. Confirm the scientific/MRV leadership model.
3. Confirm a real operational pilot partner and a technically credible pilot site.
4. Select the territorial governance/end-user replication partner.
5. Freeze the pilot problem, baseline variables, target indicators and evidence model.
6. Translate Work Packages into tasks, deliverables, person-months and eligible costs.
