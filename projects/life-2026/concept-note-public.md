# MyZubster LIFE 2026 — Public Concept Note

> Working public-safe summary derived from the internal pre-candidature Concept Note v0.2. This is not an approval, partnership, grant award or institutional commitment.

## Working title

**MyZubster LIFE — Urban Green Circularity & Water Efficiency Demonstrator**

## Problem

Urban green maintenance and territorial environmental services can rely on fragmented operational data, incomplete environmental baselines and heterogeneous procedures for recording water use, interventions, materials and maintenance.

Without a credible baseline and auditable evidence, it is difficult to demonstrate whether an operational change actually reduces water consumption, avoids material replacement, increases reuse/repair, or improves environmental performance.

## Proposed approach

The project proposes a territorial demonstrator combining real operational activity with a digital measurement and verification layer.

The working method is:

```text
BASELINE → INTERVENTION → MONITORING → VERIFICATION → COMPARISON → REPLICATION
```

The digital layer may include:

- site and asset registry;
- structured environmental/operational intervention records;
- IoT sensors and telemetry where justified;
- environmental KPI dashboards;
- audit trail and data-quality controls;
- maintenance, repairability and reuse records;
- decision-support alerts where useful;
- automation only when it can demonstrate a measurable environmental benefit.

## Primary environmental focus

- improve water-use efficiency in selected green-maintenance activities;
- demonstrate measurable water savings relative to a validated baseline.

## Complementary circularity focus

- increase reuse, repair and useful life of selected components or equipment;
- reduce avoidable replacement and associated waste where this can be measured credibly.

## Working objectives

1. Establish a robust environmental baseline.
2. Demonstrate measurable improvement in water-use efficiency.
3. Test circularity measures for selected materials/components.
4. Validate an auditable digital MRV approach.
5. Produce a transferable, open implementation toolkit for replication.

## Candidate KPI families

Quantitative targets remain **TBD until baseline collection and validation**.

Candidate KPI families include:

- water consumption per unit of area/activity;
- water saved relative to normalized baseline;
- materials/components reused or recovered;
- avoided replacement and waste;
- maintenance/useful-life performance;
- energy introduced by the pilot as an environmental guardrail;
- data completeness, continuity and validity;
- replication readiness.

## MRV requirement

Before environmental results are assessed, the project must define:

- variables and units;
- measurement frequency;
- data sources and ownership;
- quality-control rules;
- treatment of missing/anomalous data;
- baseline period and conditions;
- comparison and normalization method;
- evidence retention and auditability;
- calculation rules for each KPI.

## Role of MyZubster

MyZubster is intended to provide the digital layer for site/asset management, telemetry ingestion, API interoperability, intervention logging, KPI dashboards, audit trail, data export and an open-source replication toolkit.

The project is **not** positioned as a generic app, AI or robotics funding proposal. Technology is an enabling instrument for environmental measurement and demonstrable impact.

## Working go/no-go logic

Proceed only if the project can secure a credible pilot context, an eligible applicant structure, measurable baseline data, at least one primary environmental benefit, clear responsibilities, sustainable co-financing and a realistic replication pathway.

Redesign or stop if the proposal remains primarily a technology demonstration without quantified environmental impact.
