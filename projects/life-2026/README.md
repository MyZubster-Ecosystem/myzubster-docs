# MyZubster LIFE 2026

Public-safe working documentation for the MyZubster LIFE 2026 pre-candidature workstream.

> **Status:** pre-candidature / working material. Nothing in this directory constitutes a grant award, institutional endorsement, confirmed partnership, site authorization, funding commitment, or verified environmental result.

## Working objective

MyZubster LIFE is being developed as an environmental demonstration project focused on measurable water-use efficiency and resource circularity in real urban/territorial operations.

The intended intervention logic is:

```text
BASELINE
  → INTERVENTION
  → MONITORING
  → KPI CALCULATION
  → VERIFICATION
  → ENVIRONMENTAL RESULT
  → REPLICATION
```

The digital platform is an enabling layer rather than the environmental objective. The project must demonstrate measurable environmental benefits through traceable data and a reproducible MRV method.

## Publication boundary

This public directory intentionally excludes:

- confidential or reserved meeting briefs;
- private contact information;
- unconfirmed institutional commitments;
- negotiation notes;
- internal partner outreach drafts;
- personal employment information;
- unsupported quantitative environmental claims;
- private datasets, credentials, infrastructure details or sensitive locations.

Candidate partners, pilot hosts and institutional roles remain unconfirmed unless separately documented through formal public evidence.
