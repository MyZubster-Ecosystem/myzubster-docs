# MyZubster LIFE 2026 — Partner Landscape

> **Status:** public-safe working document. Organisations listed here are candidates or examples drawn from the internal consortium-planning material. Inclusion does not imply consent, participation, endorsement or commitment.

## Selection rule

The working decision rule is to close the four essential functions first:

```text
AUTHORITY
  → TECHNOLOGY
  → INDEPENDENT VALIDATION
  → REAL PILOT
  → REPLICATION / SPECIALIST GAPS
```

## Information required before any candidate becomes a consortium member

For every prospective beneficiary or associated partner, the working process should obtain and validate:

- legal entity name and eligibility;
- Participant Identification Code (PIC), where applicable;
- named technical owner and administrative/financial contact;
- formally accepted project role and WP/task ownership;
- proposed tasks, deliverables and milestones;
- indicative person-months and budget envelope;
- relevant infrastructure, datasets, equipment and pilot access;
- internal approval timeline;
- procurement, subcontracting or third-party constraints;
- evidence of intention to participate, subject to the organisation's formal approval process.

## Public/private boundary

The internal Drive matrix contains outreach priorities and working judgements about likely engagement. Those fields are deliberately **not reproduced** in this public repository.

GitHub should record only:

- the role model;
- candidate categories or publicly discussable candidates;
- objective contribution needed from each role;
- confirmed status once supported by formal evidence.

Until confirmation, use language such as `candidate`, `proposed`, `under discussion` or `unconfirmed`.
