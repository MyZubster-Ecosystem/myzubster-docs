## Review rules

1. Keep every unconfirmed organization marked as candidate or TBD.
2. Do not present outreach as partnership, endorsement, funding or commitment.
3. Do not invent baselines, targets, eligibility or LIFE Project Indicator mappings.
4. Validate calls, deadlines, funding rates and application rules against official sources before submission.
5. Keep personal contact details, credentials and private correspondence out of the public repository.
6. Treat all budget figures as planning estimates until partner and eligibility validation is complete.
