## 2. WORKING TITLE

MYZUBSTER LIFE — Urban Green Circularity & Water Efficiency Demonstrator

## 3. PROJECT RATIONALE

Urban green maintenance and territorial environmental services often rely on fragmented operational data, limited environmental baselines and heterogeneous procedures for recording resource consumption, interventions and maintenance activities.

This can make it difficult to quantify whether operational changes actually reduce water consumption, material use and waste generation or improve the useful life and reuse of equipment and components.

The project proposes a demonstrator that combines real operational activities with a digital measurement and verification layer, allowing baseline conditions to be compared with post-intervention performance using auditable environmental indicators.

## 4. ENVIRONMENTAL PROBLEM

The core problem addressed is the insufficiently measured use of water and material resources in selected urban green management activities and the limited availability of replicable, auditable methods to demonstrate resource-efficiency improvements.

The project will focus on a limited number of measurable environmental outcomes rather than attempting to address every feature of the MyZubster ecosystem.

Primary environmental focus:

- improved water-use efficiency in selected green-maintenance activities;

- measurable reduction of avoidable water consumption relative to a validated baseline.

Complementary circularity focus:

- increased reuse, repair and useful life of selected equipment/components;

- reduction of avoidable material replacement and associated waste where measurable.

## 5. PROPOSED SOLUTION

The project will deploy a territorial pilot combining:

- MyZubster open-source digital platform;

- site and asset registry;

- structured recording of environmental and operational interventions;

- relevant IoT sensors and telemetry;

- environmental KPI dashboard;

- audit trail and data-quality controls;

- maintenance, repairability and reuse protocols;

- decision-support alerts where justified;

- optional automation only where it generates a demonstrable environmental benefit.

Technology is treated as an enabling instrument. The project outcome is the environmental improvement demonstrated through verified data.

## 6. PILOT APPROACH

The pilot will use the following methodology:

BASELINE → INTERVENTION → MONITORING → VERIFICATION → COMPARISON → REPLICATION.

A candidate pilot site will be selected only where there is a realistic possibility to obtain permissions, collect reliable baseline data and maintain sufficient continuity of operations to compare environmental performance over time.

Futura Società Cooperativa Sociale is currently considered only as a potential operational stakeholder to be approached for a preliminary discussion. No role, partnership, site availability or LIFE participation is considered confirmed until formally agreed and eligibility/authorisations are verified.

## 7. SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES

SO1 — Establish a robust environmental baseline for selected urban green maintenance activities.

SO2 — Demonstrate a measurable improvement in water-use efficiency using monitored interventions and a documented comparison method.

SO3 — Test circularity measures for selected materials, components or equipment through maintenance, reuse, repair and replacement avoidance where technically meaningful.

SO4 — Validate a digital MRV approach that makes environmental results auditable and reproducible.

SO5 — Produce an open and transferable implementation toolkit that can be replicated in at least one additional territory or organisation subject to partner confirmation.

## 8. EXPECTED RESULTS — TO BE QUANTIFIED AFTER BASELINE

No quantitative result is claimed at this stage. Targets will be established only after baseline collection and technical validation.

Expected result categories include:

- reduced water consumption per unit of surface/activity;

- verified water savings relative to baseline;

- increased reuse or repair of selected components/materials;

- reduced avoidable replacement and waste generation;

- improved completeness and traceability of environmental data;

- validated replication protocol for additional sites.

## 9. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

KPI 1 — Water consumption per unit of area/intervention.

Possible units: litres/m² or m³/site over a defined observation period.

KPI 2 — Water saved relative to baseline.

Methodology must normalise for relevant variables such as weather, seasonality, site size and vegetation type.

KPI 3 — Materials/components reused or recovered.

Measured by mass, item count or another technically justified unit.

KPI 4 — Avoided new material/replacement and associated waste.

Requires a documented system boundary and calculation method.

KPI 5 — Maintenance and useful-life performance of selected equipment/components.

Measured through maintenance actions, repairs, avoided replacements and operational availability where applicable.

KPI 6 — Energy consumption introduced by the pilot solution.

Used as a guardrail to ensure environmental gains are not offset by disproportionate energy demand.

KPI 7 — Data completeness, continuity and validity.

Percentage of valid data, monitoring continuity and traceability of interventions.

KPI 8 — Replication readiness.

Evidence-based ability to transfer the method to additional sites or organisations.

10. MRV — MEASUREMENT, REPORTING AND VERIFICATION

An MRV protocol will be defined before environmental results are assessed. It will specify:

- variables measured;

- units and measurement frequency;

- source of each dataset;

- responsible data owner/operator;

- quality-control rules;

- treatment of missing and anomalous data;

- baseline period and baseline conditions;

- comparison and normalisation methodology;

- evidence retention and auditability;

- calculation rules for each environmental KPI.

Where necessary, a scientific/university or qualified environmental partner will support methodology and validation.

## 11. ROLE OF MYZUBSTER

MyZubster is expected to provide the digital layer for:

- site and asset management;

- environmental observation and telemetry ingestion;

- API/sensor interoperability;

- intervention logging;

- KPI dashboards;

- audit trail;

- data export and documentation;

- open-source replication toolkit.

The architecture and maturity of each component used in the pilot will be documented before final submission.

## 12. EVA IONI / AUTOMATION

EVA IONI is not presented as the environmental objective of the proposal.

Any automation or robotic functionality will be included only if:

- technical maturity is documented;

- safety and maintenance requirements are addressed;

- energy/resource consumption is measured;

- the environmental benefit can be compared against a baseline or conventional process;

- operational responsibilities are clear.

Otherwise, EVA IONI will remain outside the core LIFE scope.

## 20. CORE POSITIONING

MyZubster LIFE is not a proposal to fund a generic app, AI system or robotic platform.

It is a proposal to demonstrate, through a controlled and auditable territorial pilot, whether a digital open-source measurement and intervention framework can deliver measurable improvements in water-use efficiency and resource circularity in real urban environmental-service operations, and whether that approach can be replicated in other territories.
